# ADR 0000: Short decision title

- Status: Proposed
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Owners: project maintainers
- Roadmap links: relevant section or phase
- Supersedes: none

## Context

Describe the forces, constraints, threats, and decision deadline. Separate
known facts from assumptions that require measurement.

## Decision

State the decision precisely, including what is deliberately left open.

## Consequences

List important benefits, costs, operational effects, and security limitations.
Do not turn best-effort behavior into a guarantee.

## Alternatives considered

Record credible alternatives and why they were not selected.

## Validation and reversal

Define evidence needed to accept the decision, failure signals, and a practical
reversal path. Link measurements or test artifacts when available.
